About the app

Hebrew For Beginners is the Hebrew For Beginners video course as an app: the Hebrew alphabet, the vowel signs, reading practice, vocabulary, first sentences, handwriting and extra materials, with videos, reading lessons, recordings and quizzes. It adds three endless practices: Infinite Alphabet Practice, Infinite Reading Practice and Infinite Handwriting Practice. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or later.

Sections 1 to 3 (Introduction, The Hebrew Alphabet and Pronouncing The Vowel Signs) are free. One purchase unlocks Sections 4 to 10 and the three practices. It is a one-time purchase that unlocks them permanently on every device signed in to your Apple Account. No subscription.

Common questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All videos, readings, quizzes, pictures and recordings are inside the app, so the whole course works without an internet connection. Nothing needs to download on first launch. The app is large (about 850 MB) because the videos are included, so your iPhone may ask before downloading it over a cellular connection.

How do I keep my progress on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on both devices. The app saves your progress on the device and syncs it through your own iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes, and it waits until the device is online. There is no separate app account.

I bought the full course. How do I unlock it on another device?

Use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases. The course usually unlocks by itself. If it does not, open Settings & About (the gear on the Home screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

Can my family share the purchase?

No. The full-course purchase does not support Family Sharing.

The practice did not play a sound.

In the three practices the recording plays after you answer, never before, and Replay plays it again. Recordings play even when the ring/silent switch is on silent; check the volume of your device.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is granted, Sections 4 to 10 and the three practices lock again on your devices once the App Store reports it. Your progress is kept.

What happens if I switch to a different iCloud account?

Each iCloud account keeps its own progress. Switching back to the earlier account brings its progress back.
